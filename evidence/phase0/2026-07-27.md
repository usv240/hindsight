## Confirmation routes and threshold disclosure

The deterministic JSON example and the recorded point-in-time fixture intentionally isolate two independent confirmation routes for the same planted defect mechanism. The deterministic example sets `deterministic_cutoff_proof: true` and `point_in_time_advantage_collapsed: false`; the recorded fixture proves the separate reconstruction route. They are complementary artifacts, not two claims about one execution.

The strict-majority rule (`advantage_retained <= 0.5`) is a visible, configurable demo policy rather than a universal scientific boundary. Its measured margin is 5.142 percentage points. It cannot confirm leakage without the already-established directional post-outcome lineage and availability-time violation.

The observed AUC of `1.000000` is expected because the synthetic planted leak is total by construction. Real leakage can be subtler; the generator remains frozen rather than being retuned for a more natural-looking score.
